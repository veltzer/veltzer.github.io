A running backlog of post ideas. The recent batch shares a temperament:
reality is stranger and harsher than our consoling stories; we
systematically flatter ourselves; the uncomfortable truth is the more
impressive one. Move items to "Done" (with the post slug) as they ship.

## Proposed

Add new ideas under a heading that names the angle, with suggested tags in
italics at the end, following the entries in Done below.

### The case that connection to reality is worth having

Two of the posts from the audio-note batch end by admitting this is assumed
rather than argued — `connected_to_reality_over_time` and
`three_stages_of_religion` both say so in as many words. It is the premise
almost everything else in that batch rests on: that being tethered to facts is
better than not, for its own sake and not merely because the alternative is
embarrassing. Nothing on the blog makes the case directly.
*epistemology, philosophy*

### Untethered — humanity's disconnection from reality

Sketched in `brevity_is_not_truth` but not developed. The working title was a
book: how far human belief floats free of anything checkable, why reality
television is the ironic case, and what the internet did to the mechanism that
used to kill unsupported ideas by loneliness.
*epistemology, culture*

### Are we steering, or drifting?

`are_we_steering_or_drifting` is the sketch; the real work is the
domain-by-domain accounting it only gestures at. Law, science funding, media,
education, corporate structure, international institutions — each asked whether
it increases or decreases our collective capacity to act deliberately. The
argument is the aggregate.
*politics, philosophy, history*
